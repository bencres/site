Twenty-two drafts

The same person, drawn twenty-two different ways. Every illustration is a live Rive file, generated from hand-jittered ink and watercolor.

  1. MarginsBig italic serif, a stippled dandelion you can make a wish on, and little doodles that live in the margin.
  2. SketchbookA quiet column under an ink-and-watercolor scene, with washes that bloom in as you read.
  3. Night plumDark ink-wash: plum branches grow in from the edges and blossoms open around the words.
  4. DeskA drawn desk as the index. Every object on it opens a part of the site.
  5. HelloA hand-drawn me that watches your cursor and answers questions.
  6. HarborAlmost no words: a loose pen-and-watercolor sketch of a building on the water, with the site written into its margins.
  7. WinterDraft 3 in daylight: spruce, birch and maple grow in from the edges, snow sits on the boughs, and a chickadee hops about.
  8. RingsA tree slice as the map. Growth rings are years, and each part of the site lives somewhere in the wood.
  9. HerbariumA pressed-flower book you page through: one specimen and one section per spread.
  10. NookA second watercolor building, quiet and cozy: soft washes, warm windows, slow little movements.
  11. Winter IIWinter, pushed further: deeper light and atmosphere, more craft in every bough.
  12. Winter IIIWinter, pushed further a different way: a more immersive scene and more life in the woods.
  13. SeasonsThe winter woods through a whole year. The site follows today's date, and a slider lets you scrub from winter to winter.
  14. Seasons IIThe year woods, refined: water at the shore, a cabin whose windows glow with the hour, and a second slider for time of day.
  15. Sketchbook IIDraft 2's hillside, finished: the ink draws itself on, washes flood in and dry lighter, and the season and hour quietly follow your clock.
  16. ShireA round green door in a grassy hill: the site is the inside of the home, with lit windows at dusk and blossom, leaves or snow by season.
  17. Field notesA naturalist's field journal: a lane, hedge and gate that ink themselves in, with margin sketches that follow your season and hour.
  18. ManuscriptThe site as his own travel book in progress: "in which…" chapters, a hand-inked map of the road so far that draws itself, and a last line still wet on the page.
  19. HearthStep inside the round-doored house: a panelled hall by the fire, the kettle on, a cat in the armchair, and the season through the round window.
  20. The laneA walk down a painted country lane: a fingerpost for the nav, hand-lettered boards for each section, and your own gate at the end.
  21. Shire IIThe hobbit-hole drawing of 16, navigated by the wooden signpost from 20, with a few ink splotches and scratches.
  22. Journey mapWhat I've been up to, drawn as a pen-and-ink map: each experience is a place, and a dashed red path links them in order.
